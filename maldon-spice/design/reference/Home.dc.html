Our story

Maldon Spice is run by Jhual and Shamema, who have worked in restaurants for more than thirty years.

Grills are marinated in yoghurt and spices, then baked on skewers in a clay oven over charcoal. There are vegan specials, a dairy-free menu and a full allergen guide.

Indian food reached Maldon in 1978, and the town's first Indian restaurant opened in this very building.
