Key dates

916
Edward the Elder builds a fort and mint on the hill.
991
The Battle of Maldon. Brythnoth is killed.
1085
Domesday Survey: Maldon second only to Colchester.
1171
Charter granted under Henry II.
1576
The Corporation buys the Moot Hall.
1704
Thomas Plume leaves his library and funds a school.
1797
The Chelmer and Blackwater Navigation opens.
1847
The railway arrives.
1895
The Promenade opens.
1978
Indian cuisine comes to Maldon; the first Indian restaurant opens at 111 High Street.

Maldon Spice on the High Street

Maldon Spice is owned and run by Jhual Hafiz and his family. Jhual has more than thirty years in restaurants and ran Maldon Tandoori before opening here at 111 High Street, where Maldon's first Indian restaurant opened in 1978.

He is a former Mayor of Maldon and a serving town councillor.
